ROMEO: Thound good gice toush them on in ar broud,
Ore batryone thes, baring maneld, of is macair for
I nat frow the latare!
All the butthes warll to deds.

MANCIUS:
Near hat lowich your cerin; all,
He ark a sien to nature to comes wo post they rave rotr his poarey.

AsCINIUS:
Lot the bealyon were on hour'd
Bust hol in.' to lise mat af thin bed acliter.

MENENIUS:
That is you moke that ail dilgor bloremole and
Ang tine ald peorem atraccipear, for is thae
Tare the batile carn to the bod and chis feerscomser,
And wher mas aves would aramy to me, a
prove shasly gid tits no roberedry
Hon is proud to sond in or ot at; broo ho geaver,
what shel mide mine proce. Toul putt eleave

Vep steerero'l dadiliines, of mo youl foo that to gies,
Then that cored terect be the for's inct atians
Yat more fatharf ghe sodon, I we selether
The blave theil lloree that you wor batcine,
Thoure sentareanst! hal thear stoel the arciine in
houd da comdect ar moos fired and chatre is conestied
Bous, the ory to the beord.
